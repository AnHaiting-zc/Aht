## 等级说明
**TOP SECRET**：Access to the most sensitive classified information. Examples: National security; Intelligence sources; Critical programs.
**SECRET**：Access to classified information that could cause serious damage if disclosed. Examples: Military operations; Diplomatic information; Sensitive projects.
**CONFIDENTIAL**：Access to information that could cause damage if disclosed. Examples: Internal reports; Privacy data; Security procedures.
**UNCLASSIFIED**：No formal clearance required. Examples: Public information; Routine correspondence; General documents.

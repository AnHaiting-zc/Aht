# 文字转录与待核验｜CASE-0012

## 标题与署名
- Royal Station Hotel
- Forres, N.B.
- Innovation School, The Glasgow School of Art
- Researched and designed by Suraj Suresh, Shashank Gedala

## 可辨事件
- 1800s：Charles Edward Fraser of Sanquhar was the initial land owner.
- 1866：Opening of John Taylor's Railway & County Hotel；John Taylor购地并建造酒店；Rhind of Inverness was the architect.
- 1868：Head of Bacchus；雕塑由Mr Goodwillie of Elgin雕刻，置于酒店入口上方。
- 22 Jun, 1870：Spinks of Inverness purchased for £1850.
- 23 May, 1871：James Squair & George Bain Mackintosh purchases it.
- 10 Dec, 1872：Hotel purchased by Bailie James Campbell of Gellion's Hotel, Inverness.
- 1873：James Campbell renamed the hotel to Campbell's Royal Hotel from Taylor's Railway & County Hotel.
- 1879：Forres Cycling Club；图面称Prince Christian与Princess Helena（Queen Victoria's daughter）曾到访，因此酒店获得“Royal”称号。
- 约1880：George Washington Wilson拍摄酒店照片。
- 13 Nov, 1889：Alexander Macdonald acquired the hotel and renamed it the Royal Station Hotel.
- 1899：酒店年收入£2,155；图面称约相当于2023年的£354,000。
- Year 1903：Transfer of license from Alexander Macdonald to William Macdonald, Pitlochry.
- Mar 29, 1905：Alexander Macintosh bought the hotel.
- Mar 31, 1909：John Macdonald of the Huntly Hotel purchased The Royal.
- Year 1921：Change in ownership to Martha Crozier Macintyre & Eliza Crozier Macintyre.
- 24 Nov, 1921：George Alexander Clyne与Margaret Stronach在酒店举行婚礼。
- 12 Nov, 1952：John James Macdonald & Elizabeth Jane Macdonald purchased the hotel for £12,000.
- 26 May, 1960：The Silver Beatles苏格兰巡演期间入住；图面称次日离开时未支付账单。
- 01 Nov, 1961：Hugo Kay Kennedy purchased for £15,000.
- Oct 1965：Tyler Street Trainline ceased passenger operations；货运列车运行至May 1967，之后线路拆除。
- 11 Jun, 1968：Tennent Caledonian Breweries Limited was given the licenses for the bar.
- 13 Apr, 1977：James Wright Stewart (Jimmy Stewart) & Freda Cecilia Macbeth purchases for £32,000.
- 14 Mar, 1984：Anthony Arthur Orton & Glynis Catherine Orton purchases for £110,000.
- 27 Jun, 1984：Scottish & Newcastle Breweries Limited was given the licenses for the bar.
- 13 Aug, 1986：Andrew Watt Beattie & Janet McAlpine purchases for £117,250.
- 10 Dec, 1986：Brian Teasdale purchases for £123,000.
- Year 1993：The Royal takeover by MDP Hotel & Leisure.
- Year 1995：Stephen Peas[nall] & Densie Peas[nall] purchases The Royal（姓氏拼写待核）。
- 2016：Royal Hotel served as a temporary refuge for the homeless during this period.
- 2019：Glasgow School of Art began renting the building as student accommodation, ultimately acquiring the property in 2022.

## 识读原则
- 日期保留图面写法；仅在明确时标准化。
- “1800s”保留年代范围；约1880保留约数。
- 不根据姓名常识擅自修正Peas[nall]、Macintosh、Macintyre等拼写。
- 图面历史陈述不等于独立核实的史实。

## 待核验
初始土地所有者；1866开业和建筑师资料；Bacchus雕塑作者；1870—1872交易；1879王室来访及Royal称号来源；George Washington Wilson照片馆藏；1889更名；1899收入折算方法；1903—1921产权与许可；1921婚礼；1952—1995交易人名、公司、日期和金额；Beatles住宿叙述；铁路正式名称和停运时间；2016收容运营方；Glasgow School of Art于2019租用、2022取得物业的产权资料；地图、广告、照片和肖像的档案来源及授权。
